## Script

### Hook, 0:00 to 0:25. Chart on screen from frame one.

No intro, no logo, no "welcome back". You currently lose 73 percent of viewers
in this window.

> "These are equal highs on NQ. Here is PPI. And here is price running straight
> through them and reversing.
>
> If you were short at that level, you got stopped out on the way up. If you
> bought the breakout, you got stopped out on the way back down. Both sides
> lost, in about four minutes.
>
> That was not the news. That level was going to get taken with or without PPI,
> and I am going to show you why, so the next time you see two matching highs
> you know which side of it to be on."

Why this works: it names the loss the viewer has personally had, it removes the
obvious explanation (the news), and it promises a rule rather than a recap.

### Part 1: what equal highs actually are. 0:25 to 2:30

The reframe, stated plainly and early.

- Draw two matching highs. Ask what most traders see. Answer: resistance, a
  place to sell.
- Then say the correction: "It is not a ceiling. It is a shelf with money
  sitting on top of it."
- Keep this section short. The payoff is in Part 2, and dwelling here is where
  a definitional video would lose to JeaFx.

### Part 2: why the money sits above. 2:30 to 5:00

This is the section that earns the video. Draw it, do not just say it.

Above equal highs there are two groups of resting buy orders:
- Traders who sold at the level, whose stop losses are buy orders
- Breakout traders with buy stops waiting for the level to break

Then the point that makes it click:

> "If you need to sell a large position, you need someone to sell to. The
> buyers are all sitting above that level. So price goes up to where the buyers
> are, fills the sells, and then goes where it was always going."

Say explicitly that this is not a conspiracy, it is just how large orders get
filled. That honesty is your brand and it separates you from the hype channels.

### Part 3: the NQ case study. 5:00 to 8:00

Now the event, and only now.

- Show the equal highs forming, before PPI. Mark them.
- Point out what was knowable in advance: the level existed, the liquidity was
  above it, and a high-impact release was scheduled.
- Roll forward through the release. Show the sweep. Show the reversal.
- Say the honest part: "You cannot know it will happen. You can know where it
  would happen if it does, and that is enough to stop being the liquidity."

### Part 4: how to actually trade it. 8:00 to 10:15

The part almost nobody includes, and the reason people will finish the video.

- Do not short at the equal highs. That is the trade the move is designed to
  take out.
- Wait for the sweep, then look for displacement back through the level. That
  candle closing back below is your signal, not the wick.
- Where the stop goes: above the sweep high, not above the equal highs.
- Show one where it worked and say what would have invalidated it.

### Close. 10:15 to 11:00

- One sentence: equal highs are a target, not a ceiling.
- One instruction: "Pull up your chart, find the last set of equal highs, and
  check what price did when it reached them. It will not be random."
- One CTA, low key, the playbook. Do not stack three.
