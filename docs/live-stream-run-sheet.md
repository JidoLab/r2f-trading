# Live stream run sheet (London session)

Print this. Keep it next to the keyboard for the first two weeks.

Times are Bangkok. Subtract 6 hours for London until 25 Oct 2026, then 7.

## Why the structure matters

On the recorded videos, 73 percent of viewers left inside the first 25 seconds
and the channel gets almost no suggested traffic because of it. Live streams
are judged the same way: YouTube watches how long people stay, then decides
how many more people to show the stream to. A stream where nothing is said
for ten minutes loses everyone who just arrived. The job during quiet
stretches is to teach, not to wait.

## 1:15 PM  Prep (15 min, off stream)

- Charts open: NQ 1H, 15m, 5m, 1m. Mark yesterday's high and low, Asia high
  and low, any equal highs or lows, the nearest unfilled fair value gaps.
- Write three lines on paper: bias, the level that flips the bias, the
  liquidity pool most likely to be taken first. These are the first thing
  said on stream.
- Mask on the account balance. Notifications off. Phone on silent.
- Studio: today's stream scheduled, thumbnail up, title correct. Copy the
  watch link.
- Telegram: post the link (template at the bottom).

## 1:45 PM  Go live on the Starting Soon scene (F1)

Hit Broadcast > YouTube. Mic muted. Fifteen minutes of the Starting Soon
screen lets YouTube notify subscribers and lets early viewers gather. Use
the time to re-check the levels.

## 2:00 PM  Switch to Live (F2). The first 60 seconds

Say this within the first ten seconds, no greeting first:

> "NQ, London open. Bias today is [long/short] while price holds above/below
> [level]. First target is the liquidity at [level]. Here is why."

Then thirty seconds on the chart explaining the three lines from the paper.
Only after that: "Morning everyone, this is Harvest, welcome in."

Reason: a viewer clicking in from the notification decides in five seconds
whether this is worth it. The chart read is the reason to stay. A "hey guys,
let me get set up" is the reason to leave.

## 2:00 to 2:30 PM  The open

- Narrate what price is doing against the plan every two to three minutes,
  even when nothing is tradeable: "Still ranging under the Asia high, no
  displacement yet, so I wait."
- First setup: say the plan out loud before clicking. Entry, stop, target,
  why. Then click.
- If the trade loses, say so straight away and mark it on the chart. A loss
  handled calmly is the most trusted moment on any trading stream.

## 2:30 to 4:30 PM  The session

Rotate three things so the stream never goes silent:

1. **Manage the trade** if one is on. Say what would make you exit early.
2. **Teach off the live chart** when flat. Pick whatever is in front of you:
   why that wick took the equal highs, what a fair value gap is and where the
   nearest one sits, why the stop went there. Two to four minutes each. This
   is the material that gets clipped into Shorts later.
3. **Read chat** between setups. Say the name, repeat the question in one
   line so the replay makes sense, answer it. If it is a question about a
   trade you are in, answer after the trade.

Every 20 minutes, one line for whoever just arrived:

> "If you just came in: bias is [x], I am [flat/long/short] from [level],
> the free playbook with the exact setups is in the description."

That line is the reason the badge on screen exists. Do not say it more than
three times an hour or it sounds like a commercial.

## What to do when it is dead

The market goes quiet most days between 3:15 and 4:00 PM Bangkok. Do not sit
in silence. Options, best first:

- Walk through the last trade again on the replay bar. What was seen, what
  was missed.
- Pull up the higher timeframe and explain where price is likely to go this
  week.
- Answer the backlog of chat questions.
- Take a five minute Break (F3) and say when you will be back. Five minutes,
  not twenty.

## 4:30 PM  Ending (F4)

Three sentences on the Live scene first: what the session did, what you took,
what you learned. Then switch to Ending and say:

> "Same time tomorrow, 8 AM London. Playbook is in the description, and if you
> want it explained one to one the coaching link is there too. See you then."

Hold the Ending screen 30 to 60 seconds. Stop the XSplit output.

## 4:35 PM  After the stream (10 min)

1. Studio > Content > Live. Open today's replay. Change the title to what the
   session was actually about, for example
   "NQ Sweeps the Asia High Then Reverses: Live ICT Trade Breakdown | 12 Sep".
   The replay is a normal video from now on and needs a normal title.
2. Pin a comment with the playbook link and the time of the best trade in the
   replay ("Trade at 1:12:40").
3. Note one moment worth clipping into a Short (a clean entry, a loss handled
   well, a two minute concept explanation). Clip it the same day if possible;
   the replay is where the Shorts come from now.
4. Telegram: "Replay is up: <link>. Best trade at 1:12:40."

## Chat rules (set once in Studio > Settings > Community)

- Block links on.
- Slow mode 3 seconds.
- Blocked words: "signals", "dm me", "whatsapp me", "mentorship dm". These
  are the scam bots that show up on every trading stream.
- Never argue with a heckler on stream. "Fair enough" and move on.

## Telegram announcement (paste, change the link)

> Bias, liquidity, entries out loud. <link>

## First two weeks: what to ignore

Viewer count. It will be 3 to 15 for a while. The comparable channel sat at
those numbers for months before daily streams compounded. The two numbers
that matter are average view duration on the replay (Studio > Analytics >
Engagement) and whether it climbs week to week. Everything in this sheet is
aimed at that number.

## Replay title bank

The live title is generic on purpose (subscribers see it in the notification).
The replay needs a specific one within ten minutes of ending. Fill the
brackets, keep it under 70 characters, put the concept first and the date
last.

- NQ Sweeps the [Asia/Previous Day] High Then Reverses | Live ICT | [date]
- Equal Lows Taken at the London Open: Full Live Breakdown | [date]
- Why I Skipped the Breakout and Took the Retest | NQ Live | [date]
- One Loss, One Win, Same Level: NQ London Session | [date]
- Fair Value Gap Entry at the Open, Explained Live | NQ | [date]
- Order Block Held Twice: Live NQ Trade and the Reasoning | [date]
- No Trade Day: Why Sitting Out Was the Trade | NQ Live | [date]
- [News event] Day on NQ: Where the Liquidity Went | Live | [date]
- Stopped Out Then Re-entered: Live NQ Decision Making | [date]
- Bias Was Wrong by 8:15: How I Flipped It Live | NQ | [date]

Rule: the title states what happened on the chart. Never "Crazy day" or
"You won't believe". Concept words (liquidity, fair value gap, order block,
equal highs) are what the search traffic types.
